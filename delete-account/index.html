Delete Your Account

You can request permanent deletion of your Poultry BAZAR account and personal data at any time, whether or not you have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to poultrybazarbd@gmail.com from any email address.
  2. In the email, include the mobile number registered on your Poultry BAZAR account, and write "Delete my account" in the subject.
  3. We verify the request against the account and process the deletion within 7 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, and password
  • Your profile information

What may be retained, and for how long

  • Your published ad listings may be retained in our records for up to 90 days after account deletion, for fraud-prevention and dispute-resolution purposes, then permanently removed.
  • We do not retain any payment information, because Poultry BAZAR does not process payments — buyers and sellers transact directly by phone.

See our full Privacy Policy for more detail on how we handle your data.
