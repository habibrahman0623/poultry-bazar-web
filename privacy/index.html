Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Poultry BAZAR ("we", "our", "the app") is an online marketplace that connects poultry sellers and buyers in Bangladesh. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Poultry BAZAR mobile app and website (poultrybazarbd.com), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We only collect information that is needed to operate the marketplace:

  • Account information: your name, mobile number, and a password (stored in encrypted/hashed form, never in plain text) when you register.
  • Listing (ad) information: details you choose to publish about poultry for sale — bird count, average weight, age, health status, farm/village name, location (division, district, upazila/thana), and photographs you upload.
  • Contact number on listings: the mobile number shown on your ad so interested buyers/sellers can contact you directly by phone.
  • Location (optional): if you allow it, we use your device's approximate location only to help pre-fill the division/district/upazila fields when you post an ad. You can decline this and select your location manually.
  • Anonymous usage data: a random device identifier generated on your device (not linked to your account or name) that lets us count how many distinct devices use the app each day. No personally identifiable information is attached to this identifier.
  • Feedback and reports: messages you voluntarily submit through the in-app feedback or "report an ad" forms.

2. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not process or store any payment, card, or bank details. Poultry BAZAR does not handle any money — buyers and sellers negotiate and transact directly by phone, outside the app.
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs (e.g. no Google AdMob, no Firebase Analytics). All usage counting described above is done on our own server.
  • We do not access your contacts, SMS, camera roll (beyond the photo you explicitly pick to upload), or microphone.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, and to let you log in securely.
  • To publish your poultry listings and display them to other users searching the marketplace.
  • To show your contact number on your own listings so buyers/sellers can reach you.
  • To send one-time passwords (OTP) for account verification and password reset, via SMS to your registered mobile number.
  • To detect abuse, spam, and fraudulent activity (for example, rate-limiting repeated failed login attempts).
  • To understand overall app usage (e.g. how many devices are active) so we can improve performance and reliability.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The details you choose to put on a published ad (bird/farm details, location, photos, and your contact number) are, by design, publicly visible to anyone using the marketplace — that is the purpose of a listing. We do not share your account password, or any information beyond what you publish on your ads, with third parties, except:

  • Our SMS gateway provider, solely to deliver OTP verification codes.
  • Our cloud hosting/storage providers, solely to host the app's backend and the photos you upload.
  • Where required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and ad history for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping and fraud prevention. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below.

6. Children's privacy

Poultry BAZAR is intended for adult poultry farmers, traders, and buyers, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
